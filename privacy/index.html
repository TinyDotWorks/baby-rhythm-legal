Baby Rhythm Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-29 · 简体中文

Baby Rhythm is a local-first app. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you.

What the app stores, and where

Baby names, birth dates, feeding records (time, type, amount, nursing duration) and your settings are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. They never leave your device through Baby Rhythm.

Notifications and Live Activity

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device, and the Live Activity is shown by the system on your Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and paired Apple Watch. Both are generated on the device without any server. Live Activity content does not include baby names.

Backups

Your device's own backup (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include the app's data, as it does for other apps. That is controlled by your Apple settings, not by Baby Rhythm.

Deleting your data

Delete individual records or a baby profile inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Not medical advice

Feeding predictions are calculated only from your own records and are not medical advice.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for parents and caregivers, not for children. Because no data is collected, we hold no information about children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/TinyDotWorks/baby-rhythm-legal.

Baby Rhythm 隐私政策

生效日期 2026-09-29

Baby Rhythm 是本地优先的 App。它不会收集、传输、出售或分享任何个人数据。App 没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计分析、没有广告，也不含第三方 SDK，不会追踪你。

App 存储什么、存储在哪里

宝宝姓名、出生日期、喂养记录（时间、方式、奶量、亲喂时长）和你的设置只保存在你的设备上、App 的私有存储中，Baby Rhythm 不会把它们发送出设备。

通知与实时活动

提醒是在设备上安排的本地通知；实时活动由系统显示在锁定屏幕、灵动岛和配对的 Apple Watch 上。两者都在设备上生成，不经过任何服务器，实时活动内容不包含宝宝姓名。

备份

设备自身的备份（例如 iCloud 备份或电脑备份）可能像其他 App 一样包含本 App 的数据，这由你的 Apple 设置控制，而不是 Baby Rhythm。

删除数据

可在 App 内删除单条记录或宝宝档案，或删除 App 以清除设备上的全部数据。我们不持有任何副本，因此无需向我们提出删除请求。

非医疗建议

喂养预测仅根据你自己的记录推算，不构成医疗建议。

儿童隐私

本 App 面向家长和照护者，而非儿童。由于不收集任何数据，我们不持有关于儿童的信息。

变更与联系

政策如有变更，会在此页面发布并更新生效日期。如有疑问，请在 github.com/TinyDotWorks/baby-rhythm-legal 提交 issue。

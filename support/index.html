Baby Rhythm Support / 支持

Questions, bug reports or feedback: please open an issue at github.com/TinyDotWorks/baby-rhythm-legal/issues. Please do not include personal or baby information in a public issue.

如有问题、错误反馈或建议，请在 github.com/TinyDotWorks/baby-rhythm-legal/issues 提交 issue。请勿在公开 issue 中填写个人或宝宝信息。

Common questions / 常见问题

Where is my data? / 数据在哪里？
Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. 只在你的 iPhone 上，删除 App 即删除数据。

No reminder or Live Activity? / 没有提醒或实时活动？
Check Settings → Notifications and Live Activities for Baby Rhythm. 请在系统设置中检查 Baby Rhythm 的通知与实时活动权限。
